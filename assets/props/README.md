# Props

Rocks, logs and stumps (props.cpp loads them, meshoptimizer makes their LODs at startup), all CC0:

- Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/models), 1k glTF: boulder_01, rock_07, rock_09, rock_moss_set_02 (rocks 10, 12, 09),
  dead_tree_trunk, dead_tree_trunk_02, tree_stump_01, tree_stump_02, rock_moss_set_01 (rocks 01, 03, 05), stone_01,
  dead_quiver_trunk.
- ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), 2K: Rock030 and Rock062 in `stone/` (colour + OpenGL normal), the tiling stone
  the rock set's rocks are textured with (they share one 1k texture between seven rocks in the scan, a smear on each).
